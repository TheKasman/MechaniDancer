# MechaniDancer

A prototype exploring Crypt of the Necrodancer's grid-based movement, built in Godot/C#.

# What do we currently have working?

- Instant grid-locked movement on keypress
- A "beat bar"... well it's a square right now, that gives a visual guide for a pulse
- A backing track i made at 12pm. It feels good to write again despite how tacky it sounds. Especially after not being able to write music for over 5 years... yeah that felt bad.
- Music is now linked to the game!
This repo is entirely for the purpose of learning and seeing what I'm actually capable of.
It's always been a dream of mine to make games, specifically writing music for them but that's difficult to break in to.

# I'm serious about this

Working on this has made me very happy. Even though it's just making prototypes based off of great games I adore.
Gotta learn from the best to be the best right?
